## 1. Method symmetry
The two thermal-representation boxes are drawn with **identical size (17 × 6.2 units),
identical white fill, identical charcoal outline weight, identical type size (9.5)**, with
a neutral italic "vs" between them. Neither is styled as crude/sophisticated or
basic/advanced. ✓

## 2. No superiority implication
Pivot banner reads "Method choice, not method accuracy." No check/cross, no arrow from
proxy to physical, no colour ranking of the two boxes, no "correct/wrong/error/corrected".
The 33.3% effect is shown as a neutral change "in both directions." ✓

## 3. Numerical correctness
- "33.3% (14/42)" — matches `proxy_vs_physical_comparison.csv` / F2 (overall reclassification).
- "in both directions" — 9 more / 5 less restrictive, shown via a blue ▲ and amber ▼ flanking the label.
- "Candidate set changed in 7 of 8 scenarios" — matches `phase3_hati_vs_baseline.csv` / F3.
Denominator 14/42 is the outdoor asset-time observation count (not assets). ✓

## 4. "Both directions" visible
Yes — explicit text "in both directions" plus the two-direction markers (▲ blue = more
restrictive, ▼ amber = less restrictive), the same categorical hues as F2. ✓

## 5. Candidate-set consequence correct
Stage 4 shows the constraint-first screen (gate chips) branching to **two equal-weight
outcomes**: "Surviving alternatives" and "No defensible alternative"; footer gives the 7/8
consequence. The no-alternative box is not styled as failure/alarm. ✓

## 6. No routing / product framing
No "routing", "app", "dashboard", "platform", "HATI", or tool imagery. Reads as a
tourism-screening argument. ✓

## 7. Excluded content
The 64.3% noon result is **absent** (per spec, too easily misread without construct
context). The Abstract text is not pasted in. ✓

## 8. Text budget
Visible words (excluding numeric labels 33.3%, 14/42, 7 of 8 and acronyms SOLWEIG/Tmrt/UTCI):
**41 words** — within the ≤45 limit. ✓

## 9. Dimensions / formats
Landscape 2656 × 1062 px PNG (aspect 1 : 2.5), exceeding the TMP minimum 531 × 1328 px;
master SVG (text kept as text) + PDF; readable at ~5 × 13 cm. Nothing clipped after the
Stage-4 rebalance. ✓

## 10. Accessibility / grayscale
Meaning is carried by text labels and layout, not colour: the only colour is the two
direction markers, which are redundant with the "in both directions" text and the ▲/▼
shapes. Legible in grayscale. High contrast charcoal-on-white. ✓

## 11. Decorative clutter
No shadows, gradients, 3D, icons implying correct/incorrect, skyline, or dashboard chrome.
Four clean stages + pivot banner + footer. ✓
