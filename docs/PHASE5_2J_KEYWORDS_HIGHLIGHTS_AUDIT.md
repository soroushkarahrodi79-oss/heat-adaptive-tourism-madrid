tourism decision support · UTCI · SOLWEIG · thermal representation · spatial screening ·

**Six final keywords, one per required dimension:**

| Dimension | Keyword | Rationale |

| 2 heat/climate problem | Extreme heat | not in the title; high indexing value |

| 6 uncertainty/adaptation | Uncertainty-aware decision-making | captures the distinctive uncertainty-first architecture; adaptation already in the title ("heat-adaptive") |

Exactly **6** keywords (verified). The set is not redundant with the title: it repeats
none of "thermal representation," "opportunity screening," "heat-adaptive," or "Madrid,"
and adds "extreme heat," "UTCI," "SOLWEIG," "decision support," and "uncertainty" for
discoverability.

**SOLWEIG vs a broader tourism keyword — the trade-off.** Including SOLWEIG spends one
of six slots on the physical model rather than on a second management term (e.g.
"destination management"). It is justified: SOLWEIG is the specific method whose
sensitivity is the empirical hook, it is highly discoverable by the adjacent
urban-climate audience most likely to cite the method-sensitivity result, and tourism
identity is already secured by "urban tourism" + "tourism decision support" plus the
title and abstract. UTCI is retained as an established index with strong standalone
indexing value.
