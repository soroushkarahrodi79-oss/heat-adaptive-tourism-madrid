# GATE3B_DECISION — robustness / evidence-sufficiency verdict

**Version 1.0 · 2026-09-15.**

## Verdict

# ABSTAIN / NO ROBUST DIFFERENCE

For OD1 (Atocha → Puerta de Alcalá) on the frozen study day 2023-08-24 at the frozen
departures 14:00 and 17:00, **the open urban / meteorological / geospatial evidence is NOT
sufficient to support a robust comparative pedestrian thermal-exposure claim** that either
Route A (Paseo del Prado / Recoletos axis) or Route B (Alfonso XII park-edge) is thermally
cooler. The scientifically correct result is **ABSTAIN**.

This is a **first-class Path-B outcome**, exactly the Gate-0 research object (decision
reliability + evidence sufficiency + abstention) — not a failure.

## Basis (frozen decision rule)
1. **Sign reversal under justified perturbations (canonical ABSTAIN / NC7).** The Route A −
   Route B mean-UTCI difference **reverses sign** under ≥1 frozen perturbation at **both**
   departures: the dominant **E-P2 canopy** state (PNOA 2008–2015, and the municipal
   tree-inventory) reverses the 14:00 ordering, and **E-P5 side-of-street** reverses it at both
   14:00 and 17:00. (`GATE3B_DECISION_STABILITY.md`.)
2. **Difference within modeled uncertainty.** Baseline A−B is 0.05 °C (14:00) / 0.37 °C
   (17:00); the perturbation ensemble spans zero at both times (−1.31…+0.23 and −0.39…+0.38).
3. **Legitimate metrics disagree.** Thermal intensity marginally favours A; exposure duration
   favours B (shorter route, fewer high-stress minutes) — a preserved trade-off, not collapsed.
4. **Category-boundary proximity (NC6).** Route point-max UTCI hugs the +46 °C extreme
   boundary (45.0–45.9 °C) across perturbations.
5. **Negative controls behaved as pre-registered** (`GATE3B_NEGATIVE_CONTROLS.md`): **NC7 and
   NC4 fired**, **NC6 flagged**, **NC1 passed** (method declined to over-resolve). The
   evidence-sufficiency logic worked.

## Why not ROBUST
ROBUST requires the A−B sign preserved across all justified perturbations and clear of a
category boundary. Neither holds: canopy vintage/source and side-of-street each flip the sign,
and the difference is within modeled uncertainty.

## Claim ceiling honoured
No route is declared cooler/preferred; no accuracy, comfort, behaviour, physiological, dose, or
health claim is made. The reportable scientific claim is the **abstention itself**: with these
open data and this modeling chain, a comparative thermal-exposure decision for OD1 is **not
evidence-supported**, dominated by canopy-geometry and side-of-street uncertainty.

## What would change the verdict (future work, out of scope)

current-vintage classified canopy/crown model (collapses the E-P2 dimension); resolved
side-of-street sidewalk mapping. Absent those, ABSTAIN stands.
