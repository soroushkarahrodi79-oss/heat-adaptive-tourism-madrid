# Figure inventory and captions

The PDF embeds the four locked main figures. No figure was recalculated or redesigned for
this package. The supplementary UTCI field and graphical abstract remain available in the
repository but are not duplicated into this bounded folder.

## Main figures embedded in the PDF

1. `../../outputs/publication/figures/FIG01_STUDY_DESIGN_v0.1.png`
   - **Figure 1. Study design and constraint-first screening architecture.** The bounded
     Prado-Retiro-Atocha pilot contains 27 curated tourism assets (14 outdoor, 13 indoor).
     Two alternative thermal representations feed one ordered eligibility gate chain that
     returns surviving alternatives or an explicit no-defensible-alternative outcome.

2. `../../outputs/publication/figures/FIG02_THERMAL_METHOD_DIVERGENCE_v0.1.png`
   - **Figure 2. Thermal-method choice changes feasibility classifications in both directions.**
     Among 42 outdoor asset-time observations, 14 (33.3%) were reclassified: 9 physical more
     restrictive, 5 physical less restrictive, and 28 in agreement. Direction does not imply
     correctness or validation of either method.

3. `../../outputs/publication/figures/FIG03_SCREENING_CONSEQUENCE_v0.1.png`
   - **Figure 3. Constraint-first screening changes the option set relative to nearest-open.**
     Candidate sets changed in 7 of 8 scenarios; the nearest-open pick failed the locked screen
     in 3 of 8. S8 returned zero survivors under its specific 500 m reach constraint.

4. `../../outputs/publication/figures/FIG04_TESTED_UNCERTAINTY_v0.1.png`
   - **Figure 4. Decision robustness under tested uncertainty.** Phase 2.2 confidence counts are
     ROBUST 35, BOUNDARY 6, and UNSTABLE 1 across 42 outdoor observations. These classes cover
     only tested uncertainty and do not validate the modelled field.
