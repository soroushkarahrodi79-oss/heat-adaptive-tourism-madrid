## Submission categories (as found; verify before submitting)

ENTER27 accepts five formats: **full papers** (up to 12 pages), **working papers** (up to 5
pages), **conference presentations** (up to 2 pages), **posters and demos** (A2 format), and
**research discussion proposals** (1 page). Deadlines were reported to range September–
November 2026 across categories — `WEB VERIFICATION REQUIRED` for the exact date matching
each category, since this session could not load the official CFP page directly.

## Recommended category for HATI-Madrid

**POSTER & DEMO** — confirmed as still the right fit.

**Why:**
- HATI-Madrid is a completed, bounded pilot with locked figures and a working (if
  illustrative) Dash prototype in `app/` — exactly what a poster-and-demo format is for
  (visual results + a live/runnable artifact), rather than a full research contribution
  requiring 12 pages of new argument.
- The **full paper** track would duplicate the Tourism Management Perspectives submission
  (same study, same findings) — submitting the same core contribution as an ENTER27 full
  paper risks **self-plagiarism / prior-publication conflicts** with the journal track,
  especially since ENTER27 full papers are published in Scopus-indexed Springer proceedings
  (a citable prior-publication record that most journals, including Elsevier's, will ask
  about at submission). **Do not submit a full paper.**
- **Working paper** and **conference presentation** tracks sit in between and could work, but
  poster & demo remains the safest choice precisely because it is understood by the field as
  a preview/demo format, not a citable full contribution — minimizing overlap risk with TMP.
